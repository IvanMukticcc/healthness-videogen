READY TOPICS - the whole recipe, not just the prompt
====================================================

Each one carries only the three lines that differ per topic: the palette, the
captions and the render. Everything between them - left_disc.py, grab.py,
check_base.py, clean_poster.py - is identical for every topic and is written down
once, in flow.md. It is deliberately not repeated here: a pipeline kept in two
places drifts, and on 8 September 2026 a stale copy of the mix command in prose
would have put the finale back on the badge gain after the script was fixed.

The prompt template they all fill is in ImageSwap.txt and the opening paragraph
is identical every time: it tells the model the waves are finished and that the
right circle is not its business. Do not shorten it.

The five lifts are the argument of the clip. Pick them so the bodies differ -
five pressing movements light the same chest five times and the viewer has seen
the whole video by row two. A good set moves the red around the figure.

Check what a lift resolves to before building anything:

    ../.venv/bin/python -c "import muscle_overlay as m; print(m.resolve('auto:HIP THRUST'))"

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PICKING THE PALETTE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Rows 1, 3, 5 take the dark colour and 2, 4 the light one; the bright wave
colours go on the dark rows. Beyond that there is one rule this version adds,
and it decides the palette before anything about taste does:

  THE BADGES ARE RED, ORANGE AND BLUE, SO THE WAVES ARE NOT.

The waves live in one arc: lime, cyan, violet, fuchsia, teal. That arc was
picked by rendering a poster whose five rows carried those five hues and putting
a red, an orange and a blue badge on every one of them - all fifteen separate
cleanly, on both the dark rows and the light ones.

What is out, and why it is out rather than merely discouraged:

  amber and gold    the orange synergist badge disappears into them. This was
                    the first cut of PULL DAY, and on it the word Biceps was
                    readable only from the shadow under the ball. The synergist
                    is the commonest tier in the whole table, so an amber wave
                    loses roughly a third of every row.
  orange            the same, worse.
  red and rose      takes the primary mover, which is the muscle the row is for.
  strong blue       takes the stabiliser. Cyan is far enough away; #4FC3F7 is
                    not, and it was the second thing cut from PULL DAY.

Inside the arc, vary which end each topic sits at rather than reaching outside
it - PUSH is teal-to-violet, NO EQUIPMENT is lime-to-cyan, YOUR CORE is
fuchsia-to-cyan. Five topics that all look teal is a worse problem than one
topic that looks like another, but it is a much smaller one than a badge nobody
can read.
