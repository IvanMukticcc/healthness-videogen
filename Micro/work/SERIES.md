# What the series has already used

Every food and every organ that has been on a poster, so the next topic can be
chosen in a minute instead of an afternoon.

**This exists because the log stopped and nobody noticed.** `Prompts.txt` holds
the filled prompt for every clip up to BONE STRENGTH, which was labelled on
8 September at 12:42. Nine clips shipped after it - ALL-DAY ENERGY and parts 13
to 21 - and not one of them was written down. On 9 September choosing part 21
meant reading the captions back out of twenty-three finished posters, because
that was the only place the answer existed. The reconstruction took longer than
all nine missing entries would have taken to write.

## Where each column came from

Nothing here is remembered. Two sources, both checkable:

| column | source |
| --- | --- |
| foods, organs | the captions burnt into `<topic>_labelled.png`, read off the posters |
| palettes | measured off `base_<topic>_clean.png` - stripe medians at x<8, wave medians over the pixels that differ from the stripe by more than 60 |
| parts 5-12 and the themed clips up to BONE STRENGTH | `Prompts.txt`, which has their full prompt |

**The two sources were checked against each other.** For the eleven topics that
have both a `Prompts.txt` entry and a labelled poster, the organ in the prompt
and the caption on the poster agree in all fifty-five rows - part 7's "red blood
cells travelling inside a small vessel" is captioned BLOOD, part 8's "the cochlea
of the inner ear" is INNER EAR, part 11's "the parotid salivary gland in the
cheek" is SALIVA. That is what makes the other twelve topics, where only the
poster survives, safe to read the same way.

**What is not here, and cannot be.** The prompt body and the badge list for parts
13 to 21 exist only in `prompt_<topic>.txt`, and nothing may read those back
(`../CLAUDE.md` rule 8). So this file records what shipped - the food, the organ
as captioned, the colour - and not how it was asked for. That is the part of the
record worth having: what a new topic must avoid is what is *on* the posters.

**Parts 1 to 4 are a hole.** `Prompts.txt` entry 9 summarises them in one line -
fifteen foods to fifteen organs, "turmeric, cherries, watermelon, walnuts,
broccoli, carrots, blueberries, yoghurt, seaweed, oats, ginger, beetroot,
sardines, lentils, pumpkin seeds - to liver, heart, kidneys, brain, lungs, eyes,
skin, gut, thyroid, pancreas, stomach, spleen, bones, muscles, bladder" - with no
row-by-row attribution, and their posters are among the four this folder cannot
remake (see `PROVENANCE.md`). Treat all thirty as spent; do not try to
reconstruct which fed which.

**The darks run from grey to vivid, and the gap is at the grey end.** HSV
saturation, measured: ALWAYS TIRED's `#1E1C1B` is the least saturated at 10.0%,
then CHOLESTEROL's `#2E2A26` at 17.4%, part 21's `#2A2F35` at 20.8%, 19 at 25.0%, 10 at 27.8% and 12 at 28.2%; the
other nineteen sit between 39% and 86%, with ALL-DAY ENERGY's near-black brown at the top. So a new topic wanting a
background that is not any of these has one clear direction and it is towards
grey - the four already there are far enough apart to read as different, but a
fifth in the 39-50% band will look like immune, 17 or 20 and there are three of
those already.

The claim this table replaced said part 21's grey was "the first" the series had
used. It is the greyest, not the first, and the number is what shows it.

## What is left

The organ pool is nearly empty and it is worth saying which way.

**Spent, by system.** Digestive: stomach, colon (twice), small intestine, villi,
duodenum, appendix, oesophagus, gut lining, liver, gallbladder, bile, portal
vein, pancreas, islets, omentum. Circulatory: heart, coronary, aorta, carotid,
valve (twice), artery wall, capillaries, veins, red cells, clotting, pacemaker.
Respiratory: lungs, bronchi, alveoli, airways, cilia, windpipe, diaphragm, rib
muscles. Nervous: brain, hippocampus, amygdala, prefrontal cortex, hypothalamus,
pineal (twice), pituitary, synapse, myelin, sciatic, vagus, spinal cord, touch,
taste, smell, balance. Senses: retina, cornea, lens, macula, optic nerve, tears,
eardrum, cochlea (twice), inner ear, ear bones. Endocrine and immune: thyroid,
parathyroid, adrenals, thymus, spleen, tonsils, lymph nodes (twice), lymph, white
cells. Urinary and reproductive: kidney, nephrons, ureters, bladder, prostate,
uterus, ovaries. Structure: bones, marrow, growth plate, hip socket, vertebra,
jaw, wrist, teeth, enamel, gums, joints, meniscus, discs, ligaments, tendons,
achilles, fascia, cartilage, collagen. Muscle and skin: thigh, calf, chewing
muscle, pelvic floor, tongue, skin, nails, hair follicle, sweat, saliva, sebum,
pigment, brown fat.

**Still free**, and each one carries a warning: the pleura and the pericardium
(both drag in the lung or the heart, which are spent), the mesentery (real, but
nobody watching knows the word), the iris (drags in the eye, which has five rows
already), the shoulder, the elbow, the foot arch, the grip, the glutes, the psoas
and the neck (all fresh, but all one nutrient story - protein and magnesium - and
`flow.md` is right that five rows of one story reads as one word repeated).

**So the honest read is that the numbered series is close to done**, and the
themed format is not. A themed clip spends five foods and no organs, and the
topics nobody has made yet - stress and cortisol, blood pressure, memory, joints,
metabolism, skin, iron and tiredness, recovery - are all still open.
