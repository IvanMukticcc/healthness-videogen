WHAT IS DIFFERENT FROM MICRO'S PROMPT, AND WHY

One thing, and it changes a paragraph rather than a word: **the right-hand
circle is not the generator's.**

Foods and Micro ask for a bowl in the left circle and an organ in the right.
Macro asks for the bowl and leaves the right circle alone, because the calorie
ring is drawn there afterwards by `macro_overlay.py` - the three macros' share
of that food's energy, in the same three colours as the badges, with the kcal in
the middle. A generated organ under a drawn ring is an organ nobody will ever
see, and a generated *anything* under it changes what the ring sits on from row
to row.

So the right circle keeps its alignment mark and the mark is wiped by the
animator: `flowanim.py --anchored` restores the clean base wherever the
generator left a mark untouched. That behaviour is documented in
`engine/README.md` as a hazard for photographic variants, where it fires on
circles the generator painted *around*. Here it is the mechanism, used on
purpose - which is why the prompt says leave it rather than saying cover it.

**Both caption bars are still used.** Left carries the food, right carries what
its calories mostly are, and both come out of `foods.py --labels` rather than
being typed - so the word under the ring and the colours in it cannot disagree.

WHY THE PROMPT LOOKS REPETITIVE

Micro's `ImageSwap.txt` carries the full account and it is worth reading before
editing this one - the camera angle stated twice, the bowl's material named in
every row, the no-text rule split in two, and the paragraph forbidding a plate
by name after a poster came back with a lavender disc under every bowl. Every
one of those lines was paid for on a poster that came back wrong, and none of
them is decoration.

Two additions belong to this folder:

- **"anything you put there will be covered and wasted"** is in the right-circle
  paragraph on purpose. Told only to leave a region alone, a generator treats it
  as a region it has no instruction about and decorates it. Told what happens to
  the work, it leaves it.
- **The wave is asked for "from its bowl to the right-hand end of the row"**
  rather than "between its bowl and its organ", which is Micro's wording and has
  no meaning here. A wave asked to end at an organ that was never drawn is a wave
  the generator gets to end wherever it likes.


WHY THERE IS NO NEGATIVE PROMPT

Removed everywhere on 8 September on the user's report: the generator sometimes
deletes what the base drew - the reserved caption bars among them - and the
separate negative field is where that comes from. The body carries all of it
already and carries it in context. Do not add one back. If a generation comes
back wrong, the fix is a sentence in the body that names the thing and its place.


AFTER GENERATING

    ../.venv/bin/python ../../engine/check_base.py <topic>_poster.png \
        --base ../INPUT/base_<topic>.png

Under about two pixels of wave drift the authored mask still fits. Above that,
regenerate rather than animate - the animation cannot correct a wave the
generator has redrawn (../../CLAUDE.md rule 9).

Then the captions, from the database rather than from your memory of them:

    ../.venv/bin/python foods.py --labels '<the five foods>'
    ../.venv/bin/python ../../engine/add_labels.py <topic>_poster.png \
        -l base_<topic>_layout.json -o <topic>_labelled.png \
        --labels '<what that printed>'
