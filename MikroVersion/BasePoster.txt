THE BASE POSTER - what the liquid layer has to satisfy so the animation is
identical every time.

The idea: the liquid is drawn ONCE and never regenerated. Every future poster
reuses that exact layer; only the bowls, the organs and the labels change. The
animation tool then needs no detection at all - the mask is authored once from
this image and loaded from a file.


WHAT MUST BE TRUE OF THE LIQUID

1. CONNECTED. One unbroken ribbon per row. It starts inside the bowl and ends
   inside the organ, touching both. Nothing floating: no droplets, no splash
   crown, no spray, no thin fingers off the tip.
2. IDENTICAL IN ALL FIVE ROWS. The same wave, the same thickness, the same
   start and end points, only mirrored in colour. Copy one row four times.
3. THICK ENOUGH. At least 60 px of height at 1080 wide, everywhere along its
   length. Thin necks are what break up under any deformation.
4. NO OVERLAP WITH TYPE. Keep at least 40 px of clear space between the lowest
   point of the ribbon and the top of the label underneath it. In the current
   poster two rows have 4 and 6 px, and that alone forced half the complexity.
5. FLAT ROW BACKGROUNDS. One solid colour per row, edge to edge. No gradient.
6. INTERNAL DETAIL IS WELCOME. Long highlights and shading along the ribbon are
   what the eye follows when the surface flows. A perfectly smooth ribbon
   animates correctly but reads as calmer - it is the one row in the current
   poster that looks weaker than the rest.


THE WORKFLOW THAT KEEPS IT CONSISTENT

Do not regenerate the whole poster each time. Build it in layers:

  layer 1   background stripes + the five liquid ribbons   <- FROZEN, reused
  layer 2   bowls, organs, labels, title, logo             <- changes per topic

Export layer 1 once at 1080x1920. Everything after that is compositing, so the
liquid is pixel-identical by construction rather than by luck. This is also what
removes the model's inconsistency from the loop entirely: the part that has to
be perfect is never regenerated.


IMAGE PROMPT FOR THE BASE LAYER

Flat vector infographic poster, 9:16 vertical, five equal horizontal rows.
In every row a single smooth liquid ribbon runs from the left edge to the right
edge of the row, in one continuous S-curve of even thickness, with clean sharp
edges and a soft lengthwise highlight along its centre. The ribbon is one solid
connected band: nothing detached, no droplets, no splash, no spray, no thin
tips. Each row has one flat matte background colour, edge to edge, no gradient.
The five ribbons are the same shape in every row, differing only in colour.
Leave the left third and the right quarter of each row empty. No text, no bowls,
no icons, no logo - only the backgrounds and the five ribbons.
