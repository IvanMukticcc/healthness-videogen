ADDING A TOPIC TO base_layer.png
================================

The base layer already contains everything that must never change: the five row
background stripes, the five liquid waves, and the Healthness logo. The waves
are the animated part, and the animation reads them from ribbon_mask.png, which
was authored from this exact file. If a wave moves by even a few pixels, the
mask no longer fits and the video breaks.

So the generator's job is only to ADD: bowls on the left, organs on the right,
a label under each, and the title at the top.

Attach base_layer.png as the input image and use the prompt below.


GEOMETRY OF THE BASE LAYER  (1536 x 2752)

  waves span x 306-1212          = 20% to 79% of the width
  row 1 wave  y  563- 798        row stripes start at 0, 865, 1220, 1595, 1952
  row 2 wave  y  931-1166
  row 3 wave  y 1295-1530
  row 4 wave  y 1658-1893
  row 5 wave  y 2020-2255
  empty header above row 1       y 0-563    (top 20% - the title goes here)
  logo                           y ~2494-2576, centred

  The bowl must cover the left tip of the wave, the organ the right tip.
  Bowls sit in the left 22% of the width, organs in the right 22%.
  Labels sit below the wave inside their own row, never on top of it.


=== THE PROMPT ===  (fill the five rows, paste with base_layer.png attached)

Use the attached image as the finished background. Add elements on top of it.

DO NOT CHANGE THE ATTACHED IMAGE. The five coloured liquid waves are already
drawn and are final: do not move them, do not redraw them, do not restyle them,
do not change their shape, their length, their thickness, their colour or their
gloss, and do not extend or shorten them. The five row background colours and
the Healthness logo at the bottom are also final and stay exactly as they are.
Add no droplets, no splashes, no spray, no sparkles, no glows, no shadows and no
particles anywhere in the image.

Add only these, composited on top:

THE TITLE IS ALREADY IN THE IMAGE. Do not add a title, a subtitle, a heading or
any text at the top of the poster. The heading you can see is final and must not
be moved, restyled, recoloured or covered.
EACH ROW HAS TWO FAINT CIRCLES, one on the left and one on the right. They mark
exactly where the bowl and the organ go. Place the bowl centred in the left
circle and the organ centred in the right circle, each large enough to cover its
circle completely, so that no part of either circle is still visible in the
finished image. The circles sit over the two ends of the wave, so filling them
puts the food and the organ in contact with the liquid.

KEEP EVERYTHING INSIDE THE SAFE AREA. Nothing - no bowl, no organ, no letter of any label, no part of the title - may come closer than one tenth of the width to the left or the right edge of the poster. A vertical social feed crops the sides, and anything in that strip is lost.

  Row 5:  bowl of <FOOD 5>      organ <ORGAN 5>      labels '<LABEL 5L>' and '<LABEL 5R>'

The bowls and organs are photorealistic and cleanly cut out, with no glow and no
cast shadow. Keep every wave fully visible between its bowl and its organ.

=== END OF PROMPT ===


NEGATIVE PROMPT, if the tool offers a separate field:

changed liquid, redrawn wave, different wave shape, moved wave, recoloured wave,
extra liquid, droplets, splash, spray, particles, sparkles, glow, halo, drop
shadow, changed background colour, changed logo, watermark


AFTER GENERATING

Check the wave before animating:

    .venv/bin/python check_base.py new_poster.png

It reports how far each wave has drifted from the base layer. Under about two
pixels the authored mask still fits. Above that, regenerate rather than animate:
the animation cannot correct a wave the generator has redrawn.
