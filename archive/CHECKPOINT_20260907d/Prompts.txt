-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ABOUT COLOURS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The wave colours and the row background colours are not in the prompt, because
they are already in base_layer.png and the prompt's job is to leave them alone.

For a different palette, recolour the base layer rather than asking the
generator for it. The same ribbon_mask.png still applies: the mask is geometry,
and recolouring does not move a single pixel. So one mask covers every colour
scheme you ever make, and each palette is one base layer file.
