ADDING A TOPIC TO base_<topic>.png   —   THE OPTIONAL PATH
==========================================================

READ THIS FIRST: most Biohacks clips do not use this file.

Both circles in this variant are drawn at render time - a lucide glyph on the
left, a dial on the right - so `./render.sh <topic> 'auto:...'` produces a
finished clip with no generator in the loop and nobody waiting on one. That is
the shipped path and `flow.md` describes it.

This file is for the topics where the left circle should be a photograph: a real
sunrise through a real window, a real hand under a cold tap. It costs the round
trip - write, paste, wait, fetch, check the waves survived, look at the circles
by eye - and it buys warmth that a line icon does not have. Decide which you are
making before you build the base, because the two want different left circles.

    photographs:   ./render.sh <topic> 'auto:...' <topic>_labelled.png --scene poster
    glyphs:        ./render.sh <topic> 'auto:...'


THE RIGHT CIRCLE IS NEVER GENERATED, IN EITHER PATH

It holds the dial, and the dial is drawn by dial.py. Five posters whose dials
disagree about where a ring starts is five instruments instead of one, and the
comparison the whole clip is about - this costs two minutes and moves that, the
next costs ten and moves this - does not survive it. An image model cannot draw
the same ring twice, so it is not asked to, for the same reason it is not asked
to redraw the wave.

  LEFT circle   what the generator fills, and the only thing it fills
  RIGHT circle  the dial. Drawn over a disc that covers the circle exactly.
                LEAVE IT ALONE
  caption bars  add_labels.py writes into them. Leave them alone
  the strip under the last row  the day bar. Nothing goes there

  The safe area is one FOURTEENTH of the width, not one tenth. The circles reach
  x 112 and x 1408, and a tenth of 1536 is 154 - so a tenth tells the model to
  keep the subject out of the very circle it is told to fill, and it follows the
  sentence over the mark. A fourteenth is 110, just outside them.

WRITING THE FIVE ROWS

A hack is an action, and an action asked for as a noun comes back as a product
shot. "Cold exposure" gives a photograph of a shower head on a white background;
"a hand held under cold running water, droplets caught mid-air" gives the moment.
Name the moment, not the category:

  morning light   sunlight coming through a window onto a kitchen counter at
                  dawn, low and orange
  cold finish     a hand held under cold running water, droplets caught mid-air
  lunch walk      a pair of feet mid-step on a pavement, seen from above
  last coffee     a cup of black coffee on a table with afternoon light across it
  lights down     a bedside lamp turned low in a dark room

That is the same lesson the food version writes down as "an organ named on its
own comes back as an object", and the exercise version as "caught at the hardest
point of the rep" - a phrase the model can nod at is worth less than a thing it
either draws or does not.

AFTER GENERATING

    ../../.venv/bin/python ../../engine/grab.py <topic>
    ../../.venv/bin/python ../../engine/check_base.py <topic>.jpeg \
        --base ../INPUT/base_<topic>.png

Under about two pixels of drift the authored mask still fits. Above that,
regenerate rather than animate: the animation cannot correct a wave the generator
has redrawn.

Then look at the right circles and the day-bar strip by eye. The wave check
covers neither. Anything the generator drew *inside* the right circle is covered
by the dial's own disc, which is drawn at the full circle radius at 0.97 alpha;
anything straddling its *edge* survives and also nudges the wave's tip, because
the ribbon geometry is computed from the poster. That case is a regeneration.
Anything in the day-bar strip is not covered by anything and is also a
regeneration - the bar is a thin track on a wide empty band, and it has no disc.
